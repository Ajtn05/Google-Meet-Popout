Meet Popout

Choose what follows you when you leave the meeting.

Checking…
What should the popout show?
Meeting stage avoids your self-view when possible.
Optional. Firefox needs a temporary background tab to do this; it closes when you return.
On by default. The button appears in the meeting and opens the controls window with a direct click.
Required setup

This drives Firefox's own auto Picture-in-Picture, which ships turned off. Open about:config and set this to true — nothing works until you do:

media.videocontrols.picture-in-picture.enable-when-switching-tabs.enabled

It is also in Settings → General → Browsing.

Not popping out?

Set this to true as a fallback. It makes Firefox skip its eligibility checks — note that it also lets other sites pop out on tab switch.

media.videocontrols.picture-in-picture.video-toggle.always-show
