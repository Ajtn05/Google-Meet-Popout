Meet Popout

Checking…
Meeting stage prefers the active speaker or presentation instead of your self-view. Shared screen falls back to the stage when nobody is presenting.
Holds the meeting tab in the background with a placeholder tab, so it keeps floating while you are in another app. Closes itself when you come back.
Required setup

This drives Firefox's own auto Picture-in-Picture, which ships turned off. Open about:config and set this to true — nothing works until you do:

media.videocontrols.picture-in-picture.enable-when-switching-tabs.enabled

It is also in Settings → General → Browsing.

Two kinds of popout

With controls — the same API Chrome's Meet uses. A real window with mic, camera and hang-up buttons. It needs a click to open (Firefox has no way to open one automatically), then stays up through tab and app switches.

Automatic — opens by itself on a tab or app switch, but is video-only. Its mute button still toggles your microphone. Camera and hang-up are keyboard shortcuts, under Add-ons → gear → Manage Extension Shortcuts.

Not popping out?

Set this to true as a fallback. It makes Firefox skip its eligibility checks — note that it also lets other sites pop out on tab switch.

media.videocontrols.picture-in-picture.video-toggle.always-show
